Prerequisites

  • 1
    Enable WinRM

    Run winrm quickconfig on the Domain Controller.

  • 2
    Domain Admin Account

    Use an account with Domain Admin privileges for full audit coverage.

  • 3
    AD PowerShell Module

    The ActiveDirectory PowerShell module must be installed on the DC.

  • 4
    Firewall Port Open

    Port 5985 (HTTP) or 5986 (HTTPS) must be reachable.
